09Active Session— breathing aura

Cream marks on the ring are past reroutes in this session, not failures — moments of return. The "I drifted" button is warm-amber, not red. Pause/end shrink to circle controls so they don't compete with the timer.

The ring is now a return record

A standard countdown ring measures one thing: time elapsed. Reroute's unique metric is times rerouted — so the ring carries both. The progress arc shows time. The cream dots are reroutes, plotted at the moment they happened. By the end of a session you have a small visual record of how often you came back.

Tone shift

"You drifted" stops being a notification and becomes a data point you can see.

10Drift / Reroute— core moment

A small route diagram tells the whole story: you were on a path, you drifted off (orange), this is where we are now (the orange ring), and we're guiding you to a calm cream node. The diagram is the metaphor — the metaphor is the brand.

The path glyph is the soul of the app

Most "you got distracted" screens use a stop-sign or a red barrier. Reroute uses a road — you start on the line, drift off it, and the screen visualises the small curve back. The orange dot is where you are right now, the cream dot is where the path picks back up. The dashed line is the way back.

Copy decisions
  • "It happens" — opens with normalising, not addressing
  • "Let's get back" — first-person plural, you're not alone
  • "What pulled you?" — agency lives in the verb (the app didn't pull, the user wasn't dragged)
  • The chip "Take a 5-minute break instead" sits below the primary — leaving is also a valid choice, just framed.

12End Session— the session shape

A session shape is the headline. Violet bars are focus, hatched amber is drift, cream dots are returns. The user instantly sees: I drifted three times, and I came back three times. The number of returns is what they earned.

Reframe the metric, reframe the feeling

The temptation at end-of-session is the "focus score" — a single number that grades you. Reroute refuses. Instead the timeline tells the whole story: every drift is visible, but so is every return. The cream dots (returns) are the brightest object on the screen, by design.

18Demo Blocking— full overlay

Instead of "BLOCKED" in red, a tiny lock dropped onto the dimmed app icon. The headline "You were almost there" reframes interruption as proximity to your goal. The "3× today" footnote turns previous returns into quiet evidence.

Block screens always feel hostile. This one shouldn't.

The hardest constraint of this screen: it appears at the worst possible moment — the user wanted Instagram, badly, right now. Most apps respond with red, capital letters, a stop sign. Reroute responds with the same warm aurora, the user's own intention quoted back, and a button that's a verb (reroute) instead of a refusal (go back).

The escape hatch matters

"Unlock for 3 minutes" is always available. A blocker that can't be opened isn't gentle — it's prison. The product trusts the user to take the break and come back.

Recap of changes
  • Display serif (Fraunces) introduced for emotional headlines — kept body type as Nunito + DM Sans for legibility
  • Signature "breathing aurora" motif appears on Splash, Active Session, and Demo Block — visual continuity across the calmest screens
  • Active Session ring carries past reroutes as cream dots — turning the unique metric into a visible, accumulating object
  • Drift screen leads with a route diagram — drift (orange dotted) curving back to the path (cream) — making the brand metaphor literal
  • End Session replaces the stat grid with a session-shape timeline — every drift visible, every return brighter
  • Demo Block reframes "BLOCKED" as "You were almost there" — proximity, not refusal
  • Light-mode pair shows the system breathing in daylight — same shapes, warmer accent (terracotta, warm gold)
  • Subtle film grain across all hero screens — the warmth the brief asked for
