Reroute
Mobile app design · 18 screens across 6 flows
iPhone · 375 × 812 · dark mode primary
Find your way back to focus. A calm, non-judgmental focus recovery app
Flow 01

Onboarding

First impression. Calm, soft and quiet — no hustle copy. Five carousel slides plant the philosophy: one thing at a time, no guilt, private by default.

Flow 02

Main tabs

The four homes the user lives in. Home leads with intention not numbers; Routines, Insights and Settings stay sparse and breathable.

Flow 03

Session flow

From setting today's intention through the active timer, the drift / reroute moment, an intentional unlock, and the end-of-session debrief. The reroute screen is the most important screen in the product — warm, never punishing.

Flow 04

Routine detail

View, edit and run a saved routine. Defaults for duration, intention template, blocked apps and active days, with a soft history strip.

Flow 05

Reports & paywall

Pro upsell stays honest — free is genuinely useful. Weekly Report is the headline pro feature; permissions onboarding is calm and explanatory.

Flow 06

Utility screens

The supporting cast. Privacy in plain English, and the demo blocking overlay that demonstrates how a real intercept feels.
